Privacy Policy for Atender Student

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Leonard Joseph D. Geposon ("we," "our," or "us") built the Atender Student mobile application as a student organization attendance and fine monitoring utility for Eastern Samar State University (ESSU) students.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Student Information: Name, student ID number, email address, program/year section, and organization membership used to authenticate and track event attendance.
  • Camera & Photos: Camera access is used strictly to scan event attendance QR codes and capture selfie verification when required. Photo library access is used only when you choose to upload a profile picture.
  • Attendance & Fine Records: Event check-in timestamps, attendance status, and fine monitoring logs.

2. How We Use & Store Information

All collected information is used solely to record and display student organization event attendance and fine statuses. Data is transmitted securely over HTTPS and stored using Supabase cloud infrastructure. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Student records are retained for academic organization record-keeping. You may request account or data deletion at any time by contacting us at the email below.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data deletion requests, contact: geposonleonard@gmail.com.
